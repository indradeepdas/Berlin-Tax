# Code Of Conduct

Berlin-Tax is a technical and compliance-sensitive open-source project. Contributors are expected to keep discussions precise, respectful, and evidence-oriented.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and professional.
- Assume good intent, but require sources for claims.
- Keep disagreement focused on facts, risk, architecture, or wording.
- Be especially careful when discussing immigration, employment, benefits, tax exposure, or personal founder circumstances.
- Redact personal data, addresses, tax numbers, authority letters, and invoices from public examples and issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Pressuring contributors to provide legal, tax, immigration, employment, or company-law advice.
- Posting private user documents, tax numbers, addresses, identity documents, or authority correspondence.
- Presenting unverified advice as authoritative.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer requests to add sources, uncertainty, or review gates.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that expose private data or create unsafe certainty. Maintainers may block contributors who repeatedly violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report a conduct problem, open a maintainer issue with minimal personal data. If the report involves private data or a security-sensitive concern, follow `SECURITY.md`.
