# Source Notes: Finanzamt Onboarding

Primary source IDs:

- `elster-fseeun`: ELSTER form page for Einzelunternehmen.
- `elster-fseeun-help`: ELSTER help for the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung.
- `berlin-tax-registration`: Berlin tax registration overview.
- `bmwk-freiberuf-gewerbe-difference`: official founder guidance on Gewerbe vs Freiberufler routing.
- `bmwk-freie-berufe`: official context for free-profession categories.
- `ustg-18`: VAT pre-registration procedure context.
- `ustg-19`: Kleinunternehmer context.

Operational notes:

- ELSTER is the primary preparation surface for steuerliche Erfassung.
- The source notes for Einzelunternehmen do not cover every entity form. A UG/GmbH workflow must verify the correct ELSTER form and notary/registration status.
- Treat revenue and profit estimates as user-provided planning assumptions.
- Always ask whether the user has cross-border customers, because VAT treatment may change.
- Treat "Freiberufler" as a review posture, not a label the agent can grant.
- Log contradictory advice as evidence to resolve, not as a source of authority.
