# Roadmap

Berlin-Tax starts with founder onboarding and early operating compliance. It deliberately avoids full tax automation.

## v0.1 Founder Onboarding Core

- Berlin Gewerbeanmeldung preparation.
- Finanzamt onboarding and steuerliche Erfassung preparation.
- Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung preparation checklist and deadline candidates.
- Invoice compliance validation.
- Kleinunternehmer workflow monitoring.
- Source registry and stale-source audit.
- Self-audit for unsafe claims and hardcoded thresholds.

## v0.2 Founder Survival Workflows

- Attachment manifest validator for accountant handoffs.
- Ledger reconciliation checks for revenue, invoice, and bank-export consistency.
- Invoice sequence validator for draft/sent/paid/booked/reported document state.
- Freelancer vs Gewerbe classification preparation, with mandatory review routing.
- ALG I plus side-business compliance preparation.
- UG/GmbH setup preparation and notary/accountant handoff.
- Annual recurring compliance calendar expansion.
- Accountant handoff package generator.

## v0.3 Sector and Life-Event Packs

- Restaurant operator setup pack.
- Cross-border contractor pack.
- Immigrant founder onboarding pack with visa/work authorization review triggers.
- Late filing or correction preparation workflow.

## Non-Goals

- No ELSTER submission automation.
- No tax return generation.
- No legal classification engine.
- No replacement for Steuerberater, lawyer, Finanzamt, Arbeitsagentur, IHK, HWK, or Berlin authorities.
