# Legal Disclaimer

Berlin-Tax is an open-source operational preparation project. It is not a law firm, tax adviser, Steuerberater, accountant, government authority, or filing service.

The repository does not provide legal, tax, accounting, immigration, employment, social security, or company-law advice. It provides workflows, checklists, validation scripts, source tracking, and preparation templates.

## No Professional Relationship

Using Berlin-Tax does not create a professional-client relationship with any contributor. No contributor is responsible for a user's filing, registration, tax position, company decision, employment status, or authority communication.

## No Filing Guarantee

Berlin-Tax does not guarantee that:

- A Gewerbeanmeldung will be accepted.
- A Finanzamt position is correct.
- An invoice satisfies all legal requirements.
- A Kleinunternehmer assessment is complete.
- A UStVA deadline is final for a user's case.
- Any authority will accept a generated checklist or handoff package.

## Required Professional Review

Users should seek qualified professional review when a matter involves:

- Material tax consequences.
- Missed deadlines or corrections.
- Uncertain classification as freiberuflich or gewerblich.
- VAT registration, exemptions, reverse charge, intra-EU services, imports, or cross-border clients.
- ALG I, visa, residence permit, or work authorization interactions.
- UG/GmbH formation, managing director obligations, payroll, or shareholder loans.
- Industry permits, regulated activities, food service, alcohol, transport, health, finance, childcare, or similar regulated sectors.

## Source Freshness

German and Berlin administrative rules change. Source metadata in this repository records when a source was last checked, not that it remains correct forever. Treat stale source warnings as blocking until reviewed.
