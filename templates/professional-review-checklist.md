# Professional Review Checklist

Mark any matching item as blocking for unsupervised workflow completion.

- Unclear Gewerbe vs freiberuflich classification.
- Kleinunternehmer eligibility, opt-out, or threshold risk.
- Cross-border services, EU customers, reverse charge, import/export, OSS, or VAT ID questions.
- Missed deadline, correction, or retroactive filing.
- ALG I, Buergergeld, visa, residence permit, or work authorization interaction.
- UG/GmbH formation, shareholder arrangement, payroll, managing director salary, or shareholder loan.
- Regulated activity, permit, food service, alcohol, transport, health, finance, childcare, security, craft trade, or similar sector.
- Any material tax amount or penalty exposure.
- Contradictory online advice, adviser comments, authority statements, or forum posts that changed the user's intended action.
- Restaurant, food, alcohol, hospitality, point-of-sale receipts, cash handling, or voucher workflows.

## Verify Before Submission

- Recheck all cited official sources after the package date.
- Confirm the user's source documents match the summarized inputs.
- Confirm the responsible authority and form path are current.
- Keep generated outputs in preparation status until a qualified reviewer or authority-specific check clears the blocking items.
